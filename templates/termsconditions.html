Terms and Conditions

Cancellation Policy
Cancelling your booking 60 days before departure does not result in cancellation charges. Cancelling 30-60 days before departure will result in a charge which is 40% of the booking price. Cancelling your booking within 30 days will result in a 100% cancellation charge.

Discount Policy
Booking in advance will result in a discount on the total booking price. If you book 90-80 days before departure, a 25% discount is applied. If a booking is made 60-79 days in advance, a 15% discount is applied. Similarly, a 10% discount is made on your booking if it is booked 45-59 days in advance.
If your booking is within 45 days of departure then there is no discount.

Privacy Policy
Sensitive data that is collected through our website is encrypted so that data cannot be intercepted through transit. The information we store on our systems to ensure a smooth booking experience is
1. Email
2. Password
3. First and Last Name
4. Any bookings made
Your information that is stored with us is used to process and manage your bookings. In addition to this, we also use customer data to generate our performance reports which help us to provide a better service based on demand.
